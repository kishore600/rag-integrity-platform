The video walkthrough

A six-minute narrated video shows two parts of RAGGuard in action: the Documents page, where you inspect every document and its index, and the Isolation page, which checks that one customer's content can never reach another. It uses the real product on demo data, so you see exactly what you will see on your own workspace. This chapter lists what is in the video, with timestamps, and points to the written chapter that explains each part in full.

Where the video is

Watch it in the product. The video plays on the Help page: choose Help and user guide at the bottom of the left menu (or in the account menu), or open the address /help (you do not need an account). It has a button for each part of the video, so you can jump to one, and a captions control in the player. The same page lists every chapter of this guide.

FileWhat it is
video/RAGGuard-walkthrough.mp4The video: 5 minutes 57 seconds, 1280 by 720, with narration.
video/RAGGuard-walkthrough.vttThe captions: every spoken sentence with its time, as a standard WebVTT file. Most video players load it next to the video, and you can also read it as plain text.

About the data. The video was recorded on a demo workspace connected to a synthetic knowledge base: three tenants (acme, globex and initech), a few hundred documents, and about 50 deliberate faults. Your numbers, names and documents will differ. The layout, the labels and the meaning of every value are the same.

Follow along on your own workspace

  1. Connect your three systems and run an audit with Run audit in the top bar.
  2. Open Documents in the sidebar. Click the Needs attention card, open the first row, and press Inspect live on the Chunks tab. Compare what you see with the video.
  3. Open Isolation. Read the banner, then press Run isolation drill. The drill is read-only, so it is safe on any workspace, and it works even when the page is limited or when you have only one tenant.
  4. Press Download evidence report (.md) and open the file.
  5. To see the limited state yourself, turn on Only audit documents found in the source (S3) under Settings, Policy, run an audit, and open Isolation. Turn the switch off again and run a full audit to get the real result back. Do this only on a small index (the demo data is tiny): on a large index a full audit can run past the hosted time limit and fail, so run ragi audit on your own computer instead (see Isolation).

Captions and sound

  • The narration is a computer-generated voice (a natural-sounding neural voice, not a person). If you cannot play sound, open the captions file: it has every sentence that is spoken, in order.
  • All the information in the video is also on screen as text, in the callout labels, and in the written chapters linked above.
  • The screenshots in this guide were captured from the same demo workspace, so they match the video.
